A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Wednesday, 25 November 2026 — tankers ablaze after hitting mines in the Strait of Hormuz, Pakistan's first astronaut reaching China's Tiangong station aboard Shenzhou-24, the Houthis claiming a strike on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery a day after a Riyadh tower strike wounded 11, Huawei's conviction on 10 of 12 counts in a Brooklyn court, Trump's proposed new tariffs on 14 trading partners, and the fuel tanker fire on Cairo's ring road that killed at least 31 — plus five more stories from the day.

`draw.py` (Pillow, deterministic) renders the page to `entry.png` (1024×1024). `main.ts` copies it to the grader's output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 draw.py
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
